AquaNode · Provable Data Governance.

Give your data a bank account. Verify every interaction.

AquaNode is a self-hosted governance layer between your data and everyone who uses it. Define who may access or change which records, under which terms. Every accepted interaction becomes part of a verifiable record. Your data can stay in your existing storage.

Discuss your use case with Tim → See how AquaNode works →

As data moves and is used across systems,
its rules and history remain tied to each one.

A file may sit in cloud storage, be changed by an application, shared with a partner, and used by an AI agent.

Each system controls access and keeps its own record, leaving no single view of who was allowed to do what, under which terms, and what actually changed.

One example: a counterparty risk record inside a bank.

One governance layer between your data and everything that uses it.

AquaNode applies the same governance layer to people, applications, and AI agents.

Before a change is accepted, AquaNode checks who is acting, what they are allowed to do, and under which terms. If accepted, the interaction becomes part of the verifiable record.

AquaNode does not run your workflow. It governs the data your workflow touches.

That can be your ERP system, a partner application, a document workflow, or your own software.

Set the terms before your data is used.
Verify what happened afterwards.

AquaNode uses a signed access grant to define the permission. The grant carries the terms under which the action is allowed. Before a write is accepted, AquaNode checks the grant against the rules governing those records.

Auditable access records

Every accepted action leaves a signed record of who acted and under which terms, independently verifiable by an auditor or partner.

Identifiable actors

People, applications, and AI agents act under their own cryptographic identity, so accepted actions stay attributable to the actor that performed them.

Contract-based access

An access grant carries the terms under which an actor may act and keeps them connected to the resulting record.

Every change is linked to the one before it.

Each accepted change becomes a signed revision, cryptographically linked to the one before it. Alter an earlier revision afterwards, and verification fails at that point.

The break becomes visible at exactly that step.

Built so anyone with the record and the relevant public keys can verify the chain, even offline and without an AquaNode account.

What AquaNode holds is your choice.

How much sits inside the node depends on how you deploy it.

Default deployment

Content + record

AquaNode can keep the content together with its verifiable record and keep both available and synced.

Notary mode

Fingerprints + identities

The content remains in your existing storage. AquaNode retains the cryptographic fingerprints and identity information needed for verification.

Either way, AquaNode can run on infrastructure you control and in the jurisdiction you choose. No public ledger is required.

When someone needs to verify a record, its fingerprints can be checked without handing over the underlying content itself.

Trust that travels with your data.
