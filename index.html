So how does this work?

Follow a contract, an invoice, or an AI agent's decision as Aqua makes each step provable:

Created

How do you know it's still the original?

Aqua gives the record a fingerprint: a code that changes whenever the record does. Whoever holds the record can confirm it still matches.

Changed

Can someone rewrite an earlier version?

Each fingerprint carries the one before it, so any edit to an earlier version breaks the line of proof at that exact step.

Signed

Did a person or an AI agent really approve it?

It’s signed with a key only the person or agent controls, so no one else can sign in their name. Unlike a handwritten signature, it can’t be copied onto another document.

Verified

Do you need the original platform to check it?

No platform to log into. No public ledger. No vendor to ask.
The record carries its own proof, even offline.

The AquaTree: a document's life as one traceable lineage. Created, then Changed, then Signed, then Verified. Each version is linked to the one before it, so the whole history can be checked from the file itself, every version traced back to its origin.

Why now.

Three shifts are making independent proof impossible to ignore.

Record integrity

May 2024

Record integrity

A signed record is not the same as a provable record.

Under eIDAS 2.0, data records in qualified electronic ledgers are legally presumed to be intact and in the correct chronological order.1

Aqua records preserve who signed, what changed, and in which order, so authorised parties can independently verify their history.

$2bn+

in SEC penalties since December 2021 for failures to maintain and preserve required electronic communications.2

1 eIDAS 2.0, Regulation (EU) 2024/1183   2 U.S. SEC recordkeeping enforcement, 2021–2024

Data portability

Sep 2025

Data portability

Provider independence requires portable proof.

The UK competition authority found that technical and commercial barriers make it difficult for cloud customers to switch providers.3 Since September 2025, the EU Data Act requires providers to remove obstacles to switching.

Your records can leave a platform. The evidence that makes them verifiable often cannot. Aqua binds that evidence to each record, so it travels with the data.

Jan 2027

the last cost barrier falls: under the Data Act, charges for moving your data out of a cloud are phased out entirely.3

3 EU Data Act, Regulation (EU) 2023/2854 · UK CMA cloud services market investigation

AI liability

9 Dec 2026

Next

AI liability

If your AI causes damage, you must be able to show what it did.

From 9 December 2026, updated EU product liability rules apply to newly placed products, including software and AI systems.4 If a company fails to disclose relevant evidence ordered by a court, the product’s defectiveness can be presumed.

With Aqua, governed AI actions leave signed, verifiable records. When accountability is challenged, the evidence is already there.

4 in 5

organisations lack mature governance for autonomous AI agents.5

4 EU Product Liability Directive 2024/2853   5 Deloitte, State of AI in the Enterprise, 2026
