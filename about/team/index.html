How Aqua was born Team

Meet the team.

Aqua is built by a focused core team, long-standing contributors, and advisors who help bring the protocol into practice.

Tim Bansemer

Founder of inblock.io · Creator of Aqua Protocol

Tim has worked in IT infrastructure since 2010, spanning enterprise network engineering and security, distributed systems, and open-source services for the public sector. As the creator of Aqua Protocol, he leads its technical direction and ongoing development.

Read how Aqua was born →

“Data needs the same rigorous accounting standards as finance.”

Background

2010–2016

Enterprise security. Four years as a network engineer, then as a security engineer at Check Point Software Technologies, consulting enterprises and government departments.

2017–2020

Distributed systems and governance. Founded inblock.io. Served as RChain’s European community ambassador, co-founded the DGOV initiative on distributed governance, and led the restructuring of the Ethereum Swarm project.

2018–2020

Public infrastructure. Consulted the German public sector on open-source alternatives to foreign tech platforms, reducing dependencies and strengthening resilience. Among them dPhoenix at Dataport, known today as openDesk.

Since 2021

Aqua Protocol. Creator and technical lead, guiding the protocol from research and its first public-sector deployment through four protocol versions into real-world implementation.
