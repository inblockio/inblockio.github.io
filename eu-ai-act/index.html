How Aqua was born Team

EU AI Act · EUSAiR

Accountable AI needs a system of evidence.

We built a system of evidence. Every action (an approval, a change, a signature) is recorded as a signed receipt of who did it and when, and that receipt travels with the data itself. Any auditor, regulator or partner can verify it independently, without access to the system that produced it.

Aqua Aqua The protocol and SDK whose evidence we check against each standard. LFDT Proof of Control LFDT: Linux Foundation Decentralized Trust Publishes Proof of Control, an open standard for proving what AI agents did. EU EU AI Act EU: European Union The EU AI Act is Europe’s AI law, with duties for record-keeping, transparency and human oversight. In 2026 we took part in EUSAiR, the EU-funded pilot sandbox for the AI Act. RFC Standards RFC: Request for Comments The internet and cryptography standards Aqua is built on, and new ones we are assessing.

How Aqua differs

The proof is built into the data,
not kept by someone you have to trust.

1
Evidence stays with the data
The evidence is embedded in the data and travels with it between organisations. It does not depend on a vendor platform, public blockchain or central audit log remaining available.
2
Independent verification
Auditors and partners can verify the evidence themselves, without relying on the sender, inblock.io, a central authority or a global ledger.
3
One approach across different types of evidence
Aqua provides a consistent way to verify documents, data and AI actions, bringing signatures, data history, AI audit trails and identity into a common evidence framework.
4
Open protocol, independent of the provider
Aqua uses an open protocol and an open-licensed verifier. Evidence remains verifiable even if an organisation stops using inblock.io.
No central authority. No global ledger. No coin. Just evidence, bound to the record.

What does Aqua capture in an evidence bundle?

The payment in the demo leaves more than a log. Every exchange between the person and the AI agent, from the request to the approval and the payment, is captured in one evidence bundle: a portable data evidence package.

It holds everything needed to verify what happened, and all the data to replay it step by step. It travels with the data, so it can be verified independently, outside the system that produced it.

The evidence tag on every step.

  • What exactly fingerprint A unique code of the exact content. Change one digit and it no longer matches.
  • Who signature Made with the sender’s own key: a person, a company or an agent.
  • When time-stamp Confirmed by an independent time service, not only the sender’s clock.
  • Under which rules template The agreed form the data must fit, such as a contractor payment.
  • What belongs together seal Links every step, so none can be removed unnoticed.

Practical steps towards EU AI Act compliance with Aqua Protocol.

Aqua Protocol is a foundation for organisations to operate AI safely and accountably. Each step an AI agent takes lands in a portable evidence package, built so regulators and auditors can check it easily, without access to your systems. That builds the bridge to adopting the EU AI Act effectively.

For stand-alone high-risk AI systems, these duties apply from 2 December 2027.

The duty What the AI Act asks What Aqua adds
  • Art. 9 Risk management Identify the risks of a system and control them over its lifetime. What Aqua adds: Each step is checked against its agreed form, a person approves the critical ones, and a refused step stays on record with its reason.
  • Art. 12 Record-keeping Record events automatically over the system’s lifetime. What Aqua adds: Every event is a signed entry linked to the one before, so a changed or missing entry shows.
  • Art. 13 Information for deployers Let deployers collect, store and interpret the logs. What Aqua adds: Built so deployers can check the logs themselves, in the browser or offline, without asking the provider.
  • Art. 14 Human oversight Let people decide not to use, override or reverse what the AI does. What Aqua adds: Each approval or rejection is signed by the person who made it, with what they were shown and when.
  • Art. 19, 26 Keeping the logs Providers and deployers keep the logs for at least six months. What Aqua adds: The evidence travels with the data, so it stays checkable wherever it is kept.
