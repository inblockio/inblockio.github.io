Accountable AI needs proof.

Europe’s AI Act makes people answer for what their AI systems do. Enforcing it, and managing AI risk, rests on evidence anyone can check. Aqua Protocol builds that evidence into every step an AI agent takes.

Today, AI evidence is a promise.

  • Logs stay with the system they describe. Whoever runs the system can change them, and nobody outside can tell.
  • Oversight is hard to show. Who approved which action, under which rules and when is rarely recorded in a form anyone else can check.
  • Evidence stops at the company boundary. Partners, auditors and authorities need proof they can check themselves, without access to your systems.

Promises don’t travel between organisations. Proof does.

What Aqua attaches to every step

What exactly · fingerprint

Who · signature

Enforcement runs on evidence.

For stand-alone high-risk AI systems, these duties apply from 2 December 2027.

  • Art. 12Record-keeping Every event is a signed entry linked to the one before, so a changed or missing entry shows.
  • Art. 13Information for deployers Deployers check the logs themselves, in the browser or offline, without asking the provider.
  • Art. 14Human oversight Each approval or rejection is signed by the person who made it, with what they were shown and when.
